# Gameplay review, 2026-09-24

**Status:** parked until the architecture groundwork (ADR-0002 Phase 0) is done. The owner agrees
with the findings. The "Brainstorm" list is open and gets settled in chat before Phase 3 starts.
**Roadmap:** [ADR-0002](../adr/0002-review-roadmap.md), Phases 3 and 5.

## How this was reviewed

The release build was played in Playwright on a desktop and a Pixel 7 through one full cycle, the
upgrade pick, and into cycle 2, alongside the PRD and the domain code.

## What works

- The 33-second loop, resurrection, the fleet's damage cap and partial repair, both tiers, all
  twelve MVP cards, missiles, The Speech, and comms are in and tested.
- The portraits and the writing are the strongest part of the game. They carry the tone the PRD
  asks for.
- Fleet stakes are real: an idle player lost 9% of the fleet in the first 9 seconds and 29% by the
  spool on Viper Pilot.

## Findings

### 1. It is not yet a survivors-style game

`DIRECTOR_CAP = 2` and `ATTACK_TOKENS = 1` (`src/domain/swarm/resurrection.ts`). At most two Raiders
are on screen, one of them shooting. They fly straight down at 55 units per second and wrap around.
ADR-0001 D13 budgets 12 to 30 Raiders. The PRD's "readable before impressive" pillar is right, but
the current numbers have cut readability down to emptiness.

**Owner decision:** the middle path. More Raiders, ramping across cycles, without frustration, and
download / refill stays the rule while the resurrection ship lives.

### 2. No enemy variety or movement

There is one enemy type and one path. The heavy Raider is in MVP scope (PRD 18) and is not built.
There are no swoops, formations, flanking, or sine paths.

**Owner decision:** build the heavy Raider, with a cap on how many are on the field.

### 3. No game feel

There is no sound, no hit reaction, and a kill makes no sound. Comfort rules forbid shake and
flashing, but these are comfort-safe and carry most of what makes a shooter feel good:

- hit-stop (a 2 to 3 frame freeze on a kill),
- knockback on the hit Raider,
- small sparks at the impact point,
- damage numbers (optional, a readability risk),
- audio: a shot, a hit, a kill, a missile, the spool, the jump (ZzFX is approved).

### 4. The "download" mechanic does not explain itself

The tell is a 20 × 2 unit bar under a grey diamond. The resurrection ship arrives in cycle 2 and
cannot be hurt until cycle 4, about two and a half minutes in, so for most of the early run the
player has no reason to link ghosts to the ship. Ideas:

- a faint line from each ghost to the resurrection ship once it is on the map,
- a "RETURNING 3s" label on the ghost,
- the kill tally on returned Raiders (PRD 6.1 already plans x1, x2, x3 scratches),
- an objective line in the HUD that changes by stage: "Survive the jump," "Find the resurrection
  ship," "Break the shield," "Clear the last wave" (in Phase 1 of the UI work),
- one comms line in cycle 1 that names the loop.

### 5. No reason to play again

Win and loss are placeholders. There is no score, no run summary, and none of the planned joke
stats ("Most-killed Raider: 14 times. Still not over it."). PRD 17's share link talks about "points"
that do not exist.

**Owner decision:** add a score and a run summary.

### 6. Content notes

- **Baltar and Roslin say the same thing.** On the pick sheet both restate the card's effect. The
  "advice is honest" rule (PRD 10.1) has flattened two voices into one. Baltar should be the
  self-interested, panicky read and Roslin the long-term one, both still true.
- **Lines with live numbers go stale.** Gaeta said "0 percent and climbing steadily" with 6 seconds
  left on the spool: the line keeps the number it was chosen with. Either shorten those lines'
  time on screen or refresh the number while the line is up.

## Brainstorm (settle before Phase 3)

1. **The swarm ramp.** What does "middle path" mean in numbers? A starting proposal: the cap goes
   from 3 in cycle 1 to about 8 by cycle 6, attack tokens from 1 to 3, and strafe tokens stay at 1
   or 2. The simulation harness (ADR-0002 3.1) should check it before a playtest.
2. **Refill stays the rule.** With a bigger cap, does each download still reserve a slot, as it
   does today? That keeps "resurrection refills, never adds," but a big cap plus fast kills could
   leave the sky full of ghosts. Test both.
3. **Movement patterns.** Which few paths read well at 270 wide: straight dive, a shallow sine,
   a swoop from the side, a pair in formation? Pick two or three, not a library.
4. **Heavy Raider cap.** One on the field at a time? From which cycle? Does it hold an attack token
   of its own, or share the pool?
5. **Score.** What earns points: kills, fleet kept, speed of the factory kill, cycles survived?
   A score that rewards guarding the fleet keeps the bodyguard build viable.
6. **Run summary.** Which stats, and which of them are jokes? It is also the page a share link
   shows.
7. **Tier numbers as data.** PRD 11 wants one JSON object per difficulty before a third tier. The
   ramp and the heavy Raider will add knobs, so this may come first. Keep the knob budget
   (ADR-0001 D9).
8. **Vengeful** (PRD 6.1, v1.1): Returned Raiders prefer the Viper. Does it belong in the same
   pass as the ramp, since both change who gets shot?

## Owner answers (2026-09-24) and what they mean in the build

1. **Swarm ramp:** the middle path as proposed. The cap climbs across cycles and attack tokens with it.
2. **Keep the sky busy:** always about 3 to 5 Raiders on screen. Downloads keep refilling, with
   different wait times so returns are staggered, and fresh waves top the swarm up when kills leave
   it thin. After the resurrection ship is gone, there are still several Raiders, but none come back.
   *To confirm (PR 3b):* the Director gets a **floor** as well as a cap. Below the floor, fresh
   Raiders spawn even while downloads are pending. A finished download never pushes the swarm past
   the cap; it waits in the queue for a free slot. When the ship dies, the swarm tops up to the cap
   once (the last wave) and then nothing new or returning appears.
3. **Movement:** a straight dive, with an occasional shallow sine. The sine share can grow with the
   cycles.
4. **Heavy Raider:** its own queue, and it never downloads (only Raiders resurrect). One or two on
   screen, from about cycle 2. *To confirm (PR 3c):* one arrives per cycle from cycle 2, at most two
   alive; about 8 HP, slower, a missile does 3 rather than a kill, and it carries its own attack
   token instead of taking one from the Raiders.
5. **Score** (to design in Phase 5): kills, more for heavy kills, damage to the resurrection ship, a
   bonus for destroying it, minus for each eject and for fleet damage. It should read as how well
   the run went.
6. **Run summary:** a separate design session; it is the thing people will share.
7. **Tier numbers as data:** yes, in a JSON file so levels and new difficulties are an edit. Built in
   PR 3a (`src/balance/tiers.json`, validated on load and in `check:content`).
8. **Vengeful** (Returned Raiders prefer the Viper): after the ramp has been played, not in the same
   pass. "The ramp" in the question is item 1: the swarm growing across cycles.

## Baseline from the simulator (PR 3a, before any retuning)

`npm run sim`, 60 seeded runs per row, 12-cycle limit, phone lane:

| tier | bot | won | lost | timeout | cycles | minutes | fleet low (median / worst) | raiders on screen | ejects per run |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Civilian Run | idle | 0% | 0% | 100% | 12 | 8.4 | 44 / 42 | 1.4 | 38.3 |
| Civilian Run | hunter | 100% | 0% | 0% | 4 | 2.6 | 75 / 60 | 0.6 | 2.0 |
| Civilian Run | guard | 23% | 0% | 77% | 12 | 8.4 | 54 / 42 | 1.0 | 14.4 |
| Viper Pilot | idle | 0% | 2% | 98% | 12 | 8.4 | 12 / 0 | 1.4 | 38.2 |
| Viper Pilot | hunter | 100% | 0% | 0% | 4 | 2.6 | 72 / 49 | 0.6 | 2.0 |
| Viper Pilot | guard | 23% | 5% | 72% | 12 | 8.4 | 36 / 0 | 1.0 | 13.7 |

What it says: a pilot who chases kills wins every run in cycle 4, in under three minutes (the target
is 6 to 8), with fewer than one Raider on screen on average. An idle pilot on Viper Pilot almost never
loses, because an idle Viper does not take the maximum damage PRD 7.3 assumes. The ramp in PR 3b is
aimed at the raiders column first, then run length.
