# 0040 — Three titles

**Date:** 2026-09-23
**Slice:** replace the Recovering card table with a short list
**Ends with:** three names, one open card, and an Apply button that starts the next cycle.

---

## What we set out to do

The table showed the joke, the effect, and both advisors on every card at once. A tap
only held a choice until the scene finished, so a second tap could change which card
the next cycle actually took.

A closed row is the name and the joke. Opening it shows the effect, then Baltar and
Roslin with their portraits. Tapping the open title again leaves it selected. Apply
counts 3-2-1, then starts the cycle, so the hand can move back to the keyboard. Refresh
the list deals the one new hand and then goes away.

The newest bonus name sits between fleet health and mute. Older bonuses are a count
beside that name. A card you already own shows the stack this pick would become.

The pause menu does not cover the sheet. Esc, Pause, and About stay out of it, and so
does losing the window. Mute stays. Phaser still stops its loop while the tab is hidden,
and a blur that leaves the tab visible does not. The 3-2-1 after Apply can still pause,
because the fight is about to start.

## What we left out

No new cards, and no change to what a card does. The scene still plays in the strip.
It just no longer holds the next cycle hostage.
