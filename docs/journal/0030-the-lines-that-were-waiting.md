# 0030 — The lines that were waiting

**Date:** 2026-09-22
**Slice:** wire the jokes that already describe the fight
**Ends with:** the radio reacting to hits, returns, the factory, a streak, a graze, a thin hull, and a dry spell.

---

## What we set out to do

The packs already had lines for moments the simulation can see. Connect those. Do not
invent a heavy Raider so its lines have something to greet.

## What we built

A fleet hit, a Raider coming back, and the factory dying were already events. They now
pick a line.

The rest is noticed beside the fight:

- Three kills inside 2 seconds.
- A Cylon round that misses, but passes inside 16 units of the Viper.
- Hull at 2 or below, once, until a repair puts it back.
- Twelve seconds of combat with no kill.
- The factory crossing 75, 50, and 25 percent, speaking the hull it actually has left.
- The spool called again at 5 seconds and at 2, replacing the earlier countdown.

A louder line still wins. A moment that loses the strip is tried again, so a spool call
does not erase a hull warning that has not been said. Flavor stays quiet at 1 hull.

## What we assumed

The numbers above. They are in the PRD so a playtest can move them. One scene per damage
band, and Starbuck answering Six, were already out of this slice.

## What we tested

A miss-ring is not a hit and not a wide miss. Three quick kills speak once. The drought
waits twelve seconds and stays quiet during Recovering. The factory percent in the line
is the ship's, not the spool's. A hull warning can replace a joke, and a later spool call
can replace the earlier one. After sixteen seconds with nothing to shoot, Tigh actually
gets a turn.
