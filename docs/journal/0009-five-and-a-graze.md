# 0009 — Five, and a graze

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Slice:** fairness pass (first)
**Ends with:** the Viper outlasting one Raider, and a near-miss that now counts.

---

## What we set out to do

Three hull and a 6-unit hitbox made the dogfight a sucker's game: you vanish, they don't. This
slice changes those two numbers together, because chasing one hides the other.

Not in this slice: mid-cycle life pickups, straight-down fire, a louder 33, strafing, or civilian
ships.

## What we built

- **Viper hull 5.** Still the same pips and `n/max`. Still a full reset at the jump. Still eject,
  never a game over.
- **Raider hitbox 8 wu.** Close to the drawn box. A path 7 units off-centre is a hit, not a ghost
  miss.

## What we assumed

- Five is enough to feel the difference without turning the Viper into a sponge. Play will say.
- Aimed fire stays. Straight-down columns are a later fairness look, after this one has been sat
  with.
- Life pickups wait for the Recovering bonus cards: rare, and they need a joke, not a floating
  heart.

## What we tested

Hull still dents and ejects, just later; a graze that 6 wu treated as a miss now counts. One new
sabotage: shrink the box back to 4.

## What is still open

1. Mid-cycle life pickups, designed with the bonus cards.
2. Straight-down fire, if bodyguarding still feels like a fast eject.
3. A quiet FTL border when 10 or 5 seconds remain.
4. Flak, strafing, a lose screen, civilian ships and their hit tells.
