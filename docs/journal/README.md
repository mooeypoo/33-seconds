# Journal

One entry per slice, written as a story rather than a spec, so it can turn into a blog post later
without archaeology.

## Where things are recorded

| Kind of thing | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Game rules | `docs/PRD.md`, plus a line in its changelog |
| Architecture decisions | `docs/adr/` |
| What we built, what we measured, what surprised us, what we changed our mind about | here |

## Format

Each entry is `NNNN-short-slug.md` and covers, in whatever order reads best:

- **What we set out to do** and what was deliberately left out.
- **What we decided** and why, including options we rejected.
- **What we measured**, with real numbers and how they were obtained.
- **What surprised us**, especially dead ends: these are the parts worth reading.
- **What we revisited** and what convinced us.
- **What is still open**, so the next entry has a starting point.

Two rules: numbers come from a measurement, not from memory, and a dead end is worth more words
than a success.
