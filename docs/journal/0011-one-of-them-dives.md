# 0011 — One of them dives

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Slice:** third slice of M3
**Ends with:** one Raider marked for the fleet, and a real dent if it gets there.

---

## What we set out to do

Raiders used to pass the civilian line and wrap. Play asked why nothing happened. Now that there
are hulls to hit, one body at a time is a dive you can intercept.

Not in this slice: flak, a lose screen, more than one strafe token, or changing aimed fire.

## What we built

- **One strafe token.** The Raider farthest from you dives. A quiet orange line and a chevron on
  the fleet mark the column. The other body still only wraps.
- **Eight integrity** if the dive reaches the line, then that Raider wraps. The cycle cap still
  holds. The same notch as a stray, on the nearest hull.

## What we assumed

- Eight is enough to feel different from a stray of one, without emptying the pool in two passes.
- Farthest-from-you means intercepting the dive is leaving the shooter. Play will say if that
  fights the aimed-fire fairness look.

## What we tested

At most one diver; it is the farther Raider; a crossing costs eight; a non-diver still wraps. One
sabotage: a token for everyone.

## What is still open

1. Flak, a lose screen.
2. Mid-cycle life pickups, with the bonus cards.
3. Straight-down fire, if bodyguarding still feels like a fast eject.
4. A quiet FTL border when 10 or 5 seconds remain.
5. A graphics pass on the dive tell (directional light or descending arrow).
