# 0004 — The 33

**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Slice:** first slice of M2
**Ends with:** a real 33-second cycle. Arriving, building, spooling, a jump fade, and Recovering
that waits for Continue.

---

## What we set out to do

M1 could fly and shoot. It did not have a clock, so it was not yet this game. This slice is the
identity: 33 seconds of combat, an 8-second spool, a jump, and a calm that does not rush you.

Not in this slice: Director, Raiders that shoot, hull and eject, resurrection, upgrade cards, or
comms scenes. Recovering's Continue is the stand-in for "the pick is chosen."

## What we built

- **`JumpCycle`**, counted in ticks. 33 seconds is 1980 ticks. Storing the clock in seconds and
  adding `1/60` each tick missed the jump: 1980 × (1/60) is not 33 in IEEE floats. The identity of
  the game should not depend on a rounding error.
- Combat continues through arriving, building, and spooling. The jump clears shots and the live
  Raider without counting a kill (pending downloads will cross a jump later). Recovering does not
  tick combat and does not auto-advance.
- A HUD clock with whole seconds and a spool bar. A jump overlay that fades, never flashes white.
  A Recovering screen with Continue and no timer.

## What we assumed

- Until cards and comms exist, Continue *is* the untimed pick. The 8-12 s Recovering scene is still
  M5.
- A jump wipes the live Raider. That is not resurrection. When ghosts exist, pending downloads
  arrive first in the next Arriving.

## What surprised us

**Float time cannot be the identity clock.** The first tests expected a jump at 33.0 seconds and
got spooling, because the accumulator was `elapsed += 1/60`. The domain already thinks in ticks.
The cycle does too now.

## What we tested

Phase boundaries at 5, 25, and 33 seconds; the jump clears the sky without a kill; Recovering still
recovering after 20 extra seconds; Continue starts cycle 2 and nothing else does; pause mid-spool
freezes the remaining seconds; Continue while paused is ignored. Two sabotages: a clock that never
reaches 33, and Recovering that calls Continue by itself.

## What is still open

1. The rest of M2: Director, enemy fire, hull and eject, ghosts.
2. **A louder 33.** The current clock is enough to test the phases. Bigger type, a real FTL ring,
   and Gaeta/Dualla wait for the M5 HUD. Do not restyle it while we are still proving the rule.
3. The jump fade is a dim overlay. A ring and a proper transition wait for the art pass.
4. The hitbox revisit, still waiting on a swarm and a real phone.
