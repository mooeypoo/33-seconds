# Voice guide

Status: **Template.** Roles, triggers, and running gags are pre-filled from the PRD. Everything marked `[FILL IN]` is for the owner, and is best done in a content session.

This guide is for writers, human or AI. It is not loaded by the game. Lines that come out of it live in `src/content/` (format in `CONTENT-SCHEMA.md`).

## 1. Global

**Tone.** `[FILL IN]` One or two sentences. (Starting point: dry, affectionate, standup-flavored. The show takes itself seriously, so the game gets to sigh.)

**Who and what the jokes are aimed at.** The show's *quirks*: the fleet that always seems to be running low on something, recurring plot mechanics, everyone's dramatic timing, resurrection itself. Never the cast, the community, real people, or stereotypes.

**Spoiler cutoff.** `[FILL IN]` Lines must make sense to someone who has watched only up to: ______ (for example, the miniseries and the first N episodes). Anything past that needs the owner's explicit approval. No line implies a specific named character is a Cylon. Suspicion gags always point at the *player*.

**Catchphrases.** "Frak" is uncapped — use it wherever a line genuinely calls for it, in any speaker's voice, as often as the moment warrants. It still shouldn't carry a joke on its own; it's seasoning, not the punchline. "So say we all" is the opposite case: it's the heavier, more identifiable line, so it stays rare — once per batch, reserved for a moment that earns it (a win, a rallying beat).

**Originality.** No quotes from show scripts. Write original lines that *feel* like the show.

**Length.** 72 characters or fewer per line (see the schema). Shorter is funnier.

**Off-limits.** `[FILL IN]` Anything else you want ruled out (real events, tragedies, specific plot moments, and so on).

**Running gags (approved starting set).**
- The civilian ship count creeps up in the last seconds, and the slowest ship always makes it just in time (Dualla).
- Tyrol is overworked, and suspicious when there is no damage to fix.
- Tigh grumbles about paperwork, Vipers, and "kids these days."
- Baltar's advice is panicky, flashy, and a little self-interested. Roslin's is calm, strategic, and political.
- Gaeta reports things precisely, even absurd things.
- The player is suspected of being a Cylon. It is always a gag about the *player*.
- The most-killed Raider is still not over it.
- `[FILL IN]` Add or cut.
